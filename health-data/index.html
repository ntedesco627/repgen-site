Who we are

This is the consumer health data privacy policy of [TODO: legal entity name], [TODO: postal address] (“RepGen,” “we,” “us”). Privacy contact: support@repgen.fit.

RepGen is an iPhone app for building workouts, generating AI workouts on a schedule you set, keeping workout history, and sharing a workout one-to-one with an accepted friend. You can add an optional preset reaction. There is no free-text messaging. It is not a medical device and it does not provide medical care.

Consumer health data, under the My Health My Data Act, is personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies your past, present, or future physical or mental health status. In this app, that means the fitness information listed below. It does not mean we collect medical records.

Categories we collect, and why

We collect these categories of consumer health data, and we use each one only for the purpose stated:

  • Workouts you create. Name, exercises, sets, reps, rest, and notes. Used to store the workout and show it to you.
  • Workout history. Workouts you complete and when you complete them. Used to show your history and calendar.
  • Schedules. The days you set for AI workouts. Used to show your schedule.
  • Preferences you enter for a workout. Used when you ask for a workout.
  • A workout another person shares with you, and the sender’s name or username. Used to give you access to that workout. Sharing does not create a separate copy.
  • Information you submit to create a workout after you consent, as described in the Privacy Policy. When you ask RepGen to create a workout, we send Google the request you write and your workout settings (workout type, equipment, duration, and difficulty). For scheduled workouts we also send the names, dates, and short descriptions of your recent workouts from that schedule so the new one doesn’t repeat them. We do not send friend lists, shared workouts, reactions, your name, username, email, or account ID. Avoid including medical details in AI requests. Used only to return the workout you requested.
  • Workouts you share and preset reactions. A record of each workout you share with an accepted friend, and the reaction you pick, if any: “Try this,” “Let’s do this together,” or “Crushed it.” Used to show that share. There is no free-text messaging.
  • Reports. If a shared workout is reported, we keep a snapshot of its name and description to review the report.

If you type a symptom, injury, condition, or similar detail into a note field, we store that text as part of the workout you saved. We do not ask for diagnoses, medications, or clinical records.

We do not collect the following at launch: Apple Health or HealthKit data; precise location; biometric identifiers used to identify you; genetic data; reproductive or sexual health information; or information that identifies a health-care service you are seeking. We do not buy consumer health data.

Sources

  • You, when you enter it in the app.
  • The app, when it records a workout you mark complete or builds a calendar from what you entered.
  • An accepted friend, when they share a workout with you, with an optional preset reaction.
  • Google (Gemini API), when it returns a workout you asked it to create. Only workout creation requests are sent to Google. We do not send friend lists, shared workouts, reactions, your name, username, email, or account ID. Google’s privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. The Gemini API terms are at ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms.

We do not collect this information from data brokers or from Apple Health at launch.

Your rights

If the My Health My Data Act applies to you, you may:

  • Confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data;
  • Access your consumer health data, including a list of third parties and affiliates we have shared it with or sold it to, and an email address or other online contact for each of them;
  • Withdraw consent for our collection or sharing of consumer health data, where we rely on consent;
  • Delete your consumer health data;
  • Appeal if we deny a request.

How to submit a request: email support@repgen.fit with the subject “Consumer health data request,” and say whether you want to confirm, access, withdraw consent, or delete. To delete your account and the consumer health data in it, use Profile, then Account, then Delete Account, or email us. We may ask for information that lets us confirm the request is yours.

We respond within 45 days. If we need more time and the law allows one extension, we will tell you why within the first 45 days and respond within the additional 45 days.

Appeal. If we deny your request, email support@repgen.fit with the subject “Consumer health data appeal.” We will decide the appeal within 45 days and explain the result in writing. If we deny the appeal, we will tell you how to contact the Washington State Attorney General.

Non-discrimination. We will not deny you the app, charge a different price, or provide a different level of service because you exercised a right in this policy. We can still tell you when a feature needs information you have not given us.

Rights to access, correct, delete, port, and withdraw consent under other US state laws and under EEA and UK law are described in the Privacy Policy. Those requests also get a response within 45 days, or sooner when that law sets a shorter time.
