Who we are

This is the consumer health data privacy policy of [TODO: legal entity name], [TODO: postal address] (“RepGen,” “we,” “us”). Privacy contact: support@repgen.fit.

RepGen is an iPhone app for building workouts, generating an AI weekly plan, keeping workout history, sharing a workout with a friend you choose, and sending messages. It is not a medical device and it does not provide medical care.

Consumer health data, under the My Health My Data Act, is personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies your past, present, or future physical or mental health status. In this app, that means the fitness information listed below. It does not mean we collect medical records.

Categories we collect, and why

We collect these categories of consumer health data, and we use each one only for the purpose stated:

  • Workouts you create. Name, exercises, sets, reps, rest, and notes. Used to store the workout and show it to you.
  • Workout history. Workouts you complete and when you complete them. Used to show your history and calendar.
  • Plans and schedules. Weekly plans and the days you assign workouts. Used to show your plan.
  • Goals and preferences you enter for planning. Used to build a plan you ask for.
  • A workout another person shares with you, and the sender’s name or username. Used to deliver the copy you were sent.
  • Information you submit to create or edit a workout after you consent, as described in the Privacy Policy. When you ask RepGen to create or edit a workout, we send Google the request you write, your workout preferences [TODO: confirm fields, e.g. goals, days available, equipment, experience level], and the workout you ask it to edit. We do not send your messages, friend list, name, email, or account ID [TODO: confirm no user ID in the payload]. Avoid including medical details in AI requests. Used only to return the workout you requested.
  • Messages you send or receive. Content, time, sender, and recipient. Used to deliver the message. A message may include health details you type.

If you type a symptom, injury, condition, or similar detail into a note or goal field, we store that text as part of the workout or goal you saved. We do not ask for diagnoses, medications, or clinical records.

We do not collect the following at launch: Apple Health or HealthKit data; precise location; biometric identifiers used to identify you; genetic data; reproductive or sexual health information; or information that identifies a health-care service you are seeking. We do not buy consumer health data.

Sources

  • You, when you enter it in the app.
  • The app, when it records a workout you mark complete or builds a calendar from what you entered.
  • Another RepGen user, when they choose to share a workout with you.
  • Other users, when you receive a message from them.
  • Google (Gemini API), when it returns a workout you asked it to create or edit. We do not send chat messages, friend data, or profile or account information. Google’s privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. The Gemini API terms are at ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms.

We do not collect this information from data brokers or from Apple Health at launch.

Your rights

If the My Health My Data Act applies to you, you may:

  • Confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data;
  • Access your consumer health data, including a list of third parties and affiliates we have shared it with or sold it to, and an email address or other online contact for each of them;
  • Withdraw consent for our collection or sharing of consumer health data, where we rely on consent;
  • Delete your consumer health data;
  • Appeal if we deny a request.

How to submit a request: email support@repgen.fit with the subject “Consumer health data request,” and say whether you want to confirm, access, withdraw consent, or delete. To delete your account and the consumer health data in it, use Settings, then Account, then Delete Account, or email us. We may ask for information that lets us confirm the request is yours.

We respond within 45 days. If we need more time and the law allows one extension, we will tell you why within the first 45 days and respond within the additional 45 days.

Appeal. If we deny your request, email support@repgen.fit with the subject “Consumer health data appeal.” We will decide the appeal within 45 days and explain the result in writing. If we deny the appeal, we will tell you how to contact the Washington State Attorney General.

Non-discrimination. We will not deny you the app, charge a different price, or provide a different level of service because you exercised a right in this policy. We can still tell you when a feature needs information you have not given us.

Rights to access, correct, delete, port, and withdraw consent under other US state laws and under EEA and UK law are described in the Privacy Policy. Those requests also get a response within 45 days, or sooner when that law sets a shorter time.

Consent

We collect the workouts, history, plans, and messages you create because you put them into the app to use those features. Before your first AI request, we show a separate consent screen that names Google as the recipient, lists the data sent, explains the purpose, and links to this policy. Accepting the Terms does not give that consent. We do not send consumer health data to Google without that consent. We will ask for your permission before any future Apple Health connection, and we will use data you allow only for the features you turn on, never for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and we will not store it in iCloud. We do not use Apple Health at launch.

You can withdraw it anytime in [TODO: settings path] or by email at support@repgen.fit. We will stop the use that depended on consent. Withdrawal does not erase a copy a friend already received, including a message already delivered. You can also delete your account.

Children

You must be 18 or older to use RepGen. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

Changes

If we make a material change to this policy, we will notify you in the app or by email before it takes effect and, where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent. We will change the “Last updated” date when this page changes.
