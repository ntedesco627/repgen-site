Who we are

RepGen is an iPhone workout app. You can build workouts yourself or with AI, get AI workouts on a schedule you set, keep a workout history, and share a workout one-to-one with an accepted friend. You can add an optional preset reaction. There is no free-text messaging. It is not a public social network. Android may come later. This policy describes the iOS app.

Information we collect

We collect the following information when you use RepGen:

  • Account details. You can sign in with an email address or with Sign in with Apple. If you use email, we collect the email address you enter, your name if you provide it, and a username if you set one.
  • Sign in with Apple. Apple shares a unique Apple user ID, and optionally your name and email or a private relay email, with us through Firebase Authentication. We never receive your Apple ID password.
  • Workouts and fitness information you enter. Workouts you build (name, exercises, sets, reps, rest, and notes), workout history, schedules, goals, and preferences.
  • Friends and shared workouts. If you add friends, we store your friend list and friend requests. We also store the workouts you share and the preset reaction you pick, if any. Sharing only works between accepted friends.
  • Push token. If you turn on notifications, we store a device push token. Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) receives that token and the notification, which can name a workout and the friend who shared it.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe, Apple charges your Apple ID. We and RevenueCat receive subscription status (for example, whether a plan is active, which product it is, and renewal timing). We do not receive your full payment card number.
  • Support emails. If you email us, we keep the email and our reply so we can help you.

We do not connect to Apple Health or HealthKit at launch. See Apple Health for what will apply if we add that later.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create and secure your account, including email sign-in and Sign in with Apple, through Firebase Authentication;
  • store, sync, and show your workouts, history, schedules, and plans;
  • create a workout with AI when you ask, after you consent;
  • give an accepted friend access to a workout you share, with the preset reaction you pick, if any;
  • store your friend list, friend requests, the workouts you share, and the reactions you pick;
  • send a notification you turned on;
  • confirm a subscription through Apple and RevenueCat;
  • respond to support requests, review reports of a user or a shared workout, and keep the service secure;
  • comply with law and respond to valid legal process.

No sale, no ads, no tracking

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not show ads in RepGen, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

We will not use personal information for a new purpose that is materially different from the purposes above unless we notify you first and, where the law requires it, get your consent before that new use.

Sharing workouts with a friend

You can share a workout only with an accepted friend, and each share is one-to-one. Shared workouts are not posted publicly and are not visible to people you did not choose. Sharing gives that friend access to your workout (name, exercises, sets, reps, rest, and notes). It does not create a separate copy. You can add an optional preset reaction: “Try this,” “Let’s do this together,” or “Crushed it.” There is no free-text messaging. Your name or username may be shown as the source.

If you delete a workout, we delete it and your friends lose access to it. If you delete your account, friends lose access to anything you shared, as described under Friends.

You can report or block another user in the app. Blocking a user removes them as a friend and stops them from sharing workouts with you or sending you friend requests. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including under US state privacy laws and the laws of the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct information that is inaccurate;
  • Delete your personal information;
  • Port your information — receive a copy in a portable format, and where the law gives the right, ask us to send it to someone else when that is technically feasible;
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent, including consent for AI features. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before you withdrew. You can withdraw it anytime in [TODO: settings path] or by email at support@repgen.fit. You can also stop using AI features at any time, and you can delete your account.

You can view and update your profile in the app. To exercise a right, email support@repgen.fit from the address on your account, or use in-app deletion for a deletion request. We may need to confirm the request is yours. Where your state’s law allows an authorized agent to submit a request, we will accept one that meets that law’s requirements.

We respond within 45 days of receiving a request we can verify. If a law gives you a shorter deadline, we meet that shorter deadline. If you are in the EEA or the United Kingdom, we respond within one month, unless a permitted extension applies, and we will tell you if we extend. If a US state law allows us more time, we will tell you why inside the first 45 days and finish within the extra period that law allows.

Appeal. If we deny your request, you may appeal by emailing support@repgen.fit with the subject “Privacy appeal.” We will respond to the appeal within 45 days and explain the result in writing. If we deny the appeal, we will tell you how to contact your state attorney general or another regulator when the law requires that notice.

Non-discrimination. We will not deny you the app, charge you a different price, or reduce the quality of the app because you exercised a privacy right. A feature can still require the information it needs in order to work. For example, we cannot show a workout you have not created.

Children

You must be 18 or older to use RepGen. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.
