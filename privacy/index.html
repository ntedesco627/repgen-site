Who we are

RepGen is an iPhone workout app. You can build workouts yourself or with AI, get AI workouts on a schedule you set, keep a workout history, and share a workout with a friend you choose. It is not a public social network. Android may come later. This policy describes the iOS app.

Information we collect

We collect the following information when you use RepGen:

  • Account details. You can sign in with an email address or with Sign in with Apple. If you use email, we collect the email address you enter, your name if you provide it, and a username if you set one.
  • Sign in with Apple. Apple shares a unique Apple user ID, and optionally your name and email or a private relay email, with us through Firebase Authentication. We never receive your Apple ID password.
  • Workouts and fitness information you enter. Workouts you build (name, exercises, sets, reps, rest, and notes), workout history, schedules, goals, and preferences.
  • Friends and messages. If you add friends, we store your friend list and friend requests. If you send messages, we store their content, time, sender, and recipient. Messages may contain anything you type, including health details.
  • Push token. If you turn on notifications, we store a device push token. Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) receives that token and the notification text, which can name a workout and the friend who shared it. Notification text does not include message previews.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe, Apple charges your Apple ID. We and RevenueCat receive subscription status (for example, whether a plan is active, which product it is, and renewal timing). We do not receive your full payment card number.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we keep the message and our reply so we can help you.

We do not connect to Apple Health or HealthKit at launch. See Apple Health for what will apply if we add that later.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create and secure your account, including email sign-in and Sign in with Apple, through Firebase Authentication;
  • store, sync, and show your workouts, history, schedules, and plans;
  • create a workout with AI when you ask, after you consent;
  • give a friend you choose access to a workout you share;
  • store your friend list and friend requests, and deliver messages you send;
  • send a notification you turned on;
  • confirm a subscription through Apple and RevenueCat;
  • respond to support requests, review reported messages and content, and keep the service secure;
  • comply with law and respond to valid legal process.

The AI provider is Google (Gemini API). Google’s privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. The Gemini API terms are at ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms. Only workout creation requests are sent to Google. We do not send chat messages, friend data, your name, username, email, or account ID.

When you ask RepGen to create a workout, we send Google the request you write and your workout settings (workout type, equipment, duration, and difficulty). For scheduled workouts we also send the names, dates, and short descriptions of your recent workouts from that schedule so the new one doesn’t repeat them. Avoid including medical details in AI requests.

Under Google’s terms for its paid Gemini API service, Google does not use your requests or responses to improve its products; it processes them as our processor and keeps logs for a limited time for abuse detection and legal compliance. We do not use AI output to advertise to you.

No sale, no ads, no tracking

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not show ads in RepGen, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

We will not use personal information for a new purpose that is materially different from the purposes above unless we notify you first and, where the law requires it, get your consent before that new use.

Sharing workouts with a friend

You can share a workout with a friend you choose. Shared workouts are not posted publicly and are not visible to people you did not choose. Sharing gives that friend access to your workout (name, exercises, sets, reps, rest, and notes). It does not create a separate copy. Your name or username may be shown as the source.

If you delete your account, workouts you shared are deleted and your friends lose access to them.

You can report or block another user in the app. Blocking a user stops them from messaging you, sharing workouts with you, or sending you friend requests. Friends and messages

If you add friends, we store your friend list and friend requests. Chats are one-to-one. A chat is a list of shared workouts, each with an optional note. If you send messages, we store their content, time, sender, and recipient so we can deliver them. Messages may contain anything you type, including health details, so share only what you’re comfortable with. You can report or block a user from a chat or their profile. Blocking a user stops them from messaging you, sharing workouts with you, or sending you friend requests.

If you or another user reports a message or chat, we may review the reported content and recent messages in that chat to enforce our Terms. Otherwise we do not read your messages.

When you delete your account, we permanently delete your friend list, friend requests, your workouts (including ones you shared, so friends lose access to them), and the workouts and notes you sent in chats, from our active systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days. Workouts and notes the other person sent you stay in their chat history.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information, workouts, history, and related data while your account is open, so the app can show them to you.

You can delete your account in the app: Profile, then Account, then Delete Account. You can also email support@repgen.fit and ask us to delete it.

After a verified deletion request, we delete account data within 30 days. We purge remaining copies from backups within 90 days. We do not keep account data indefinitely after you delete your account. We may keep specific information longer only when a legal hold or another law requires us to keep it, and only for that purpose.

Deleting your account also deletes your friendships, your shared workouts, and what you sent in chats, as described under Friends and messages.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including under US state privacy laws and the laws of the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct information that is inaccurate;
  • Delete your personal information;
  • Port your information — receive a copy in a portable format, and where the law gives the right, ask us to send it to someone else when that is technically feasible;
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent, including consent for AI features. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before you withdrew. You can withdraw it anytime in [TODO: settings path] or by email at support@repgen.fit. You can also stop using AI features at any time, and you can delete your account.

You can view and update your profile in the app. To exercise a right, email support@repgen.fit from the address on your account, or use in-app deletion for a deletion request. We may need to confirm the request is yours. Where your state’s law allows an authorized agent to submit a request, we will accept one that meets that law’s requirements.

We respond within 45 days of receiving a request we can verify. If a law gives you a shorter deadline, we meet that shorter deadline. If you are in the EEA or the United Kingdom, we respond within one month, unless a permitted extension applies, and we will tell you if we extend. If a US state law allows us more time, we will tell you why inside the first 45 days and finish within the extra period that law allows.

Appeal. If we deny your request, you may appeal by emailing support@repgen.fit with the subject “Privacy appeal.” We will respond to the appeal within 45 days and explain the result in writing. If we deny the appeal, we will tell you how to contact your state attorney general or another regulator when the law requires that notice.

Non-discrimination. We will not deny you the app, charge you a different price, or reduce the quality of the app because you exercised a privacy right. A feature can still require the information it needs in order to work. For example, we cannot show a workout you have not created.

Children

You must be 18 or older to use RepGen. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

Apple Health, later

RepGen does not use Apple Health or HealthKit at launch. If we add that connection, we will ask for your permission before we access any Apple Health data. We will use data you permit only to provide the features you turn on. We will never use it for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and we will not store it in iCloud.
