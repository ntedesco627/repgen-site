Who we are

RepGen is an iPhone workout app. You can build workouts yourself or with AI, get an AI weekly plan, keep a workout history, and share a workout one-to-one with a friend. It is not a public social network. Android may come later. This policy describes the iOS app.

Information we collect

We collect the following information when you use RepGen:

  • Account details. If you create an account with a name, email, and username, we collect those.
  • Sign in with Apple. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us a unique Apple user ID for your account. Apple may also share your name and email address, and only if you choose to share them. The email may be the email you use with Apple, or an Apple private relay email (an address that ends in @privaterelay.appleid.com and forwards to you). If you hide your name or email, we do not receive them from Apple. We never receive your Apple ID password.
  • Workouts and fitness information you enter. Workouts you build (name, exercises, sets, reps, rest, and notes), workout history, schedules, weekly plans, goals, and preferences.
  • Push token. If you turn on notifications, we store a device push token so we can send reminders through Apple Push Notification service.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe, Apple charges your Apple ID. We and RevenueCat receive subscription status (for example, whether a plan is active, which product it is, and renewal timing). We do not receive your full payment card number.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we keep the message and our reply so we can help you.

We do not connect to Apple Health or HealthKit at launch. See Apple Health for what will apply if we add that later.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create and secure your account, including Sign in with Apple;
  • store, sync, and show your workouts, history, schedules, and plans;
  • generate an AI workout or weekly plan when you ask for one, after you consent;
  • deliver a workout to the one friend you choose to share it with;
  • send a notification you turned on;
  • confirm a subscription through Apple and RevenueCat;
  • respond to support requests, review reports of objectionable content, and keep the service secure;
  • comply with law and respond to valid legal process.

We will not use personal information for a new purpose that is materially different from the purposes above unless we notify you first and, where the law requires it, get your consent before that new use.

Sharing workouts with a friend

You can share a workout with one other RepGen user. Shared workouts are not posted publicly and are not visible to people you did not choose. The recipient gets a copy of the workout you shared (name, exercises, sets, reps, rest, and notes included in that workout). Your name or username may be shown as the source.

If you later delete that workout, or delete your account, the recipient keeps the copy they already received. Deleting your copy does not reach into their account and erase theirs.

You can report or block another user in the app. We review reports within [TODO: report review window]. We may remove content and suspend or terminate accounts that break the Terms of Use. Retention and deletion

We keep account information, workouts, history, and related data while your account is open, so the app can show them to you.

You can delete your account in the app: Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. You can also email support@repgen.fit and ask us to delete it.

After a verified deletion request, we delete account data within 30 days. We purge remaining copies from backups within 90 days. We do not keep account data indefinitely after you delete your account. We may keep specific information longer only when a legal hold or another law requires us to keep it, and only for that purpose.

A friend who already received a shared workout keeps that copy, as described under Sharing.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including under US state privacy laws and the laws of the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct information that is inaccurate;
  • Delete your personal information;
  • Port your information — receive a copy in a portable format, and where the law gives the right, ask us to send it to someone else when that is technically feasible;
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent, including consent for AI features. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before you withdrew. Email support@repgen.fit to withdraw consent. You can also stop using AI features at any time, and you can delete your account.

You can view and update your profile in the app. To exercise a right, email support@repgen.fit from the address on your account, or use in-app deletion for a deletion request. We may need to confirm the request is yours. Where your state’s law allows an authorized agent to submit a request, we will accept one that meets that law’s requirements.

We respond within 45 days of receiving a request we can verify. If a law gives you a shorter deadline, we meet that shorter deadline. If you are in the EEA or the United Kingdom, we respond within one month, unless a permitted extension applies, and we will tell you if we extend. If a US state law allows us more time, we will tell you why inside the first 45 days and finish within the extra period that law allows.

Appeal. If we deny your request, you may appeal by emailing support@repgen.fit with the subject “Privacy appeal.” We will respond to the appeal within 45 days and explain the result in writing. If we deny the appeal, we will tell you how to contact your state attorney general or another regulator when the law requires that notice.

Non-discrimination. We will not deny you the app, charge you a different price, or reduce the quality of the app because you exercised a privacy right. A feature can still require the information it needs in order to work. For example, we cannot show a workout you have not created.
